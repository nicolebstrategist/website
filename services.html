The Foundation

A 4-week introduction to the Respect Method approach.

Change is only possible when you know where you are starting from.

The Foundation is a 1:1 coaching programme designed for those who want a clear, honest audit of their current reality before committing to a longer journey. We identify what has been shaping your patterns and what needs to shift, so you can move forward with intention.

The Process

We begin with your current reality: health, habits, and context. We identify the specific triggers keeping you stuck and map out a different approach tailored to your life.

The Outcome

A personalised summary of your situation, your key patterns and triggers, and a clear set of initial directions to build on, whether independently or through The Respect Method.

What's included

  • 4 x 60-minute weekly sessions
  • Pre-programme questionnaire
  • Written session summaries
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • Final personalised written strategy

The Respect Method

A twelve-week private partnership built on the two pillars most programmes skip.

Most approaches fail because they ignore the life you actually live. They hand you a template and assume your relationship with yourself is actually fine. The Respect Method is different. It builds the programme around you, focusing on:

  • The Inner Foundation: How you speak to yourself and make decisions when no one is watching or when you feel under pressure.
  • The Real Context: The specific cultural, professional, and relational factors shaping your days.

This isn't about a lack of discipline; it’s about a lack of alignment. We don't fit you into a programme; we build the conditions that make lasting change inevitable.

What's included

  • 12 x 60-minute weekly sessions
  • Pre-programme questionnaire
  • Written session summaries
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • Bespoke wellbeing strategy built for your reality

Every session opens with a review of your week: what you committed to and what you followed through on. The structure is designed to keep you moving forward between sessions, not just during them, and to build the autonomy to do so long after the programme ends.

£900 | Payment plans available

The Growth

Ongoing support to protect your progress and sustain your evolution.

Research consistently shows that lasting lifestyle change is non-linear and requires ongoing support, particularly when life throws new challenges. Maintenance isn't a sign that the work wasn't done properly. It's a sign that you understand how change actually works.

The Growth is a flexible, ongoing coaching programme designed for those who have already built a meaningful foundation, whether through The Respect Method or another serious personal development process, and want continued support to sustain it, deepen it and keep growing.

Two sessions a month gives you enough structure to stay accountable and enough space to integrate life changes. It's not about starting over. It's about not standing still.

What's included:

  • 2 x 60-minute sessions per month
  • Written session summaries
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday

Duration is flexible and based on your evolving needs.

Investment:

£160 per month
