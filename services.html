The Foundation

A 4-week introduction to the RESPECT Method™ approach.

Change is easier when you know where you're actually starting from.

The Foundation is a 4-week 1:1 mentoring programme for people who are curious about working together but not yet ready to commit to a full 12-week journey. It gives you a clearer, more honest picture of where you are right now, what has been shaping your patterns, and what needs to shift, so that when you're ready to go deeper, you know exactly what you're stepping into.

The Process

We begin with your current reality. Your health and wellbeing, your habits, your context and your goals. From there, we start to identify the patterns that have been keeping you stuck and explore what a different approach could look like for you specifically.

The Outcome

A personalised summary of your current situation, your key patterns and a clear set of initial directions tailored to your life and context. A foundation to build on, whether that's through The RESPECT Method™ or independently.

What's included

  • 4 x 60-minute sessions per month
  • A pre-programme questionnaire
  • Written session summaries after each conversation
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • A personalised written summary at the end

The RESPECT Method™

A 12-week private mentoring partnership built on the two things most wellbeing programmes skip.

Most approaches tell you what to do. They hand you a plan, assume the relationship you have with yourself is fine, and ignore the life you're actually living.

Two things get left out almost every time:

  • The inner foundation of how you see yourself, talk to yourself, and make decisions about yourself, and
  • The real context of your life: your history, your environment, your culture, your relationships, the particular pressures shaping your days.

A plan designed for someone else, in a life that looks nothing like yours, was never going to hold. Not because you lacked discipline. Because it was never actually built for you.

The RESPECT Method™ doesn't fit you into a programme. It builds the programme around you, treating your context and inner foundation not as background detail, but as the conditions that make lasting change possible.

What's included

  • 12 x 60-minute sessions over 12 weeks
  • A pre-programme questionnaire
  • Written session summaries after each conversation
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • A personalised wellbeing strategy built around your life, not a template

Every session opens with a review of your week: what you committed to and what you followed through on. The structure is designed to keep you moving forward between sessions, not just during them, and to build the autonomy to do so long after the programme ends.

The Growth

Continued support for people who have done the foundational work and are ready to keep moving forward.

Change doesn't stop when the programme ends. Neither does the work.

Research consistently shows that lasting lifestyle change is non-linear and requires ongoing support, particularly when life throws new challenges into the mix. Maintenance isn't a sign that the work wasn't done properly. It's a sign that you understand how change actually works.

The Growth is a flexible, ongoing mentoring programme designed for people who have already built a meaningful foundation, whether through The RESPECT Method™ or another serious personal development process, and want continued support to sustain it, deepen it and keep growing.

Two sessions a month gives you enough structure to stay accountable and enough space to integrate what's shifting in your life. It's not about starting over. It's about not standing still.

What's included

  • 2 x 60-minute sessions per month
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • Access to digital resources

Duration is flexible and decided together based on where you are and what you need.

Whatever stage you're at, the starting point is always the same: understanding yourself well enough to build a life that feels like your own.
