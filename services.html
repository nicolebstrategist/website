The Foundation

Not sure if you are ready for a longer commitment? Start here.

You cannot change what you cannot see.

The Foundation is a 4-week 1:1 coaching programme built around self-knowledge, self-awareness and self-trust. Before we can change anything, we need to understand what is actually driving your choices, your habits and the way you see yourself. That is what this programme is for.

The Process

We start with where you are right now. Through guided sessions and reflection, we map your current reality — your habits, your routines, your triggers and the patterns that have been shaping your daily life without you fully realising it.

The Outcome

You leave with a clear, honest picture of yourself and your life, a better understanding of what has been keeping you stuck, and the self-knowledge to start making decisions that actually reflect who you are. Whether you continue independently or move into The Respect Method, you will not be starting from zero.

What is included

  • 4 x 60-minute weekly sessions
  • Pre-programme questionnaire
  • Written session summaries
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • Personalised written action plan

Investment:

£320 / €380

The Respect Method

A twelve-week 1:1 coaching programme to help you live more intentionally, in line with who you are and what genuinely matters to you.

Most approaches try to change behaviour without taking into account your context, your environment or the habits and dynamics that shape your daily life. The Respect Method is different.

We work across your physical wellbeing, habits, relationships, environment, mindset and decision-making, recognising that lasting change depends on how these areas influence one another. Rather than approaching them in isolation, we create practical changes that fit your reality and can be sustained over time.

This is not about perfection, relentless productivity or control. It is about developing enough self-respect to build a life that no longer asks you to abandon yourself in order to belong, succeed or keep going.

What is included:

  • 12 x 60-minute weekly sessions
  • Pre-programme questionnaire
  • Written summaries after each session
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • Personalised strategy built around your reality

Each session starts from your actual week: the decisions you made, the challenges you faced, what moved forward and what did not. The goal is not to keep you coming back indefinitely. It is to build the clarity and self-respect to keep going long after the programme ends.

Investment:

£900 / €1,050 | Payment plans available

The Growth

For those who have completed The Respect Method and want to keep the momentum going.

Change does not stop when the programme ends. The Growth is a flexible, ongoing coaching option for clients who want continued support to maintain their progress, deepen their self-knowledge and keep making intentional decisions as life evolves.

The Growth is a flexible, ongoing coaching programme designed for those who have already built a meaningful foundation, whether through The Respect Method or another serious personal development process, and want continued support to sustain it, deepen it and keep growing.

Two sessions a month gives you enough structure to stay on track and enough space to integrate what is changing in your life.

What is included:

  • 2 x 60-minute sessions per month
  • Written session summaries
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday

Duration is flexible and based on your evolving needs.

Investment:

£150 / €180 per month
