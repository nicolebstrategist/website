Last updated: May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how www.nicolebadotti.com ("Website") uses cookies and similar technologies. This Website is operated by Nicole Badotti.

We use cookies in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and, where applicable to EU visitors, the EU ePrivacy Directive. This policy has been updated to reflect the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (DUAA).

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, improve user experience and provide analytical information. Cookies cannot access other data on your device or run programs.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Essential for the proper functioning of the Website. These cannot be disabled. They enable core functions such as security, navigation, session management and your cookie consent preferences.

Analytics Cookies (Performance)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use this Website — for example, which pages are visited most, how long visitors stay and how they arrived. This data is aggregated and used to improve our Website.

Under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, certain aggregated analytics activities may be permitted without consent where they do not involve identifying individual users. However, we have implemented consent-based controls for all analytics cookies as a matter of best practice. Google Analytics cookies will only be set after you provide consent via our cookie banner.

The analytics cookies used include:

  • _ga — Distinguishes users. Duration: 2 years.
  • _ga_[ID] — Maintains session state. Duration: 2 years.

Functional Cookies

These allow the Website to remember your preferences and choices, such as your cookie consent settings, to provide a more consistent experience. Duration varies.

Targeting Cookies

We do not currently use targeting or advertising cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated and consent will be sought before any targeting cookies are placed.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by trusted third-party services we use, including:

  • Google Analytics (analytics) — Privacy Policy
  • Kit (ConvertKit) (email marketing) — Privacy Policy
  • Calendly (appointment booking) — Privacy Policy
  • Formsubmit (contact form processing) — Privacy Policy

These third parties process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

4. Legal Basis

Under UK PECR and UK GDPR, the legal basis for placing non-essential cookies is your freely given, specific and informed consent. We obtain this consent via our cookie banner before any non-essential cookies are placed. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through our cookie banner settings. You may also adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect Website functionality.

6. Cookie Retention Periods

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for the duration specified in Section 2 above unless deleted earlier. You can delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

We use your personal data only where we have a lawful basis to do so under UK GDPR. The lawful bases we rely on are as follows:
