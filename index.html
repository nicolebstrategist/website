Self-Respect and Wellbeing Strategist

I guide people beyond passivity and self-criticism towards self-respect, clarity and personal coherence, so they can reclaim authorship of their lives.

I am a polyglot and have lived in Brazil, Australia, England, the United States, Switzerland and France, spent time in Canada and South Korea, and travelled to around 50 countries. This path has shaped a multicultural perspective on how identity, environment and behaviour influence one another.

Through experience and continuous study, I have come to understand that lasting change rarely comes from quick solutions or external validation. It emerges from deliberate, consistent decisions aligned with one’s values and real-life context.

In practice, self-respect becomes the structural foundation for change, resilience and lasting wellbeing.

About

My early experience with chronic allergies and skin conditions led me to question not only symptoms, but the broader imbalances shaping my life. What began as a search for physical relief evolved into a deeper investigation of daily exposures — environmental, relational and psychological — and their cumulative impact on the nervous system and identity.

Over time, I implemented structured, evidence-informed changes to reduce toxic load, strengthen boundaries and align my habits with my values. While some conditions remain, they are now managed with clarity rather than reactivity. This journey shaped the core principle of my work: lasting wellbeing is built through conscious, coherent decisions grounded in self-respect.

Purpose

My Approach

I work from a systemic perspective, addressing the broader patterns shaping your behaviour rather than isolated symptoms. This includes environment, habits, relationships, internal dialogue and cultural context. Change becomes realistic and sustainable when decisions are aligned across these domains. Self-respect functions as the core principle of this process.

Global Perspective

A multicultural lens applied to personal strategy, grounded in context, culture and individuality.

Structured Self-Respect

Self-respect translated into deliberate decisions, clear boundaries and consistent routines aligned with life context.

Reduction of Toxins

A strategic reduction of physical, relational and behavioural toxins to support intentional and sustainable living.
