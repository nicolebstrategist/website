About

I spent a decade in luxury fashion and hospitality, across six countries and speaking six languages. I built my identity as the one who could adapt to any context, any culture, and any demand. But constant adaptation comes at a cost. It took me years to finally realise that "fitting in" everywhere had left me feeling like I belonged nowhere. Least of all, to myself.

Being made redundant from my role at MATCHES, followed by a near-collapse from burnout, forced the pause I was too afraid to take. That space taught me how to stop reacting to the expectations of others and start making decisions from a place of self-respect. Today, I guide other women to do the same: to stop merely "managing" their lives and start reclaiming authorship of them.

Purpose

To guide people to reclaim authorship of their lives through self-respect.

Vision

To live in a world where self-respect is not a luxury, but the daily standard from which people make decisions, build habits and lead their lives.

My Approach

I help you reorganise your life by looking at the whole picture — physical, mental, emotional, social, environmental and spiritual — rather than isolated symptoms. Lasting change only happens when your habits, decisions and environment are all in synchrony. Self-respect is the foundation that makes this alignment possible.

Multicultural Perspective

Context shapes behaviour. Having lived across six countries, I translate my global experience into a deeper understanding of the cultural and social pressures you face.

Self-Respect in Practice

Self-respect is not a mindset; it’s a daily practice. It is reflected in the decisions you make, the limits you hold, and how you prioritise your own needs when the world demands your attention.

Reduction of Toxins

We identify and remove the physical, relational, and behavioural toxins undermining your energy and health. Rather than adding to your load, we subtract what depletes you.
