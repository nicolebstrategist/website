About

I know what it feels like to be capable, driven and quietly exhausted. To look like you have it all together while wondering how long you can keep going at this pace.

For over a decade I worked in luxury fashion and hospitality across six countries, always adapting, always delivering, always putting everyone else first. It took being made redundant from MATCHES, and nearly burning out after that, to finally pause and turn that same attention inward.

I am a certified Health and Wellness Coach, accredited by the UKIHCA. I work with women who want to build a kind and respectful relationship with themselves, so they can start making decisions that reflect who they are and what they truly want.

The way you treat yourself
sets the standard for everything else.

Why work with me

Self-respect applied daily

Most people know what they need to do; or at least they think they do. The gap is between knowing and actually doing. I help you close that gap by developing self-awareness and self-knowledge. When you understand your own values, qualities, patterns, triggers and needs, you stop relying on motivation and willpower and start building self-trust.

Multicultural perspective

I do not just understands expat life in theory. I have lived it across six countries, navigating different cultures, expectations and versions of myself. I know what it costs to constantly try to adapt and fit in, and I know what it takes to finally stop and be honest with yourself.

Reduction of toxins

Most programmes pile on more: more habits, more routines, more pressure. My approach works differently. Before we add anything, we identify and reduce what is already depleting your energy, harming your health and keeping you stuck. Less noise. Less weight. More energy to actually change.

With the right support and structure,
progress stops feeling like a struggle and becomes second nature.
