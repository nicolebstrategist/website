About

I spent more than a decade in luxury fashion and hospitality, living across six countries. I built my identity around being adaptable, professional, and capable in any context. And I was. But constant adaptation has a cost that takes years to notice.

Being made redundant from the luxury retailer MATCHES and having left another role on the verge of burnout, was the turning point. I was forced to stop and look honestly at how I was living and how I had been treating myself. It was only through becoming aware of my patterns and practising self-respect daily that I was able to change the narrative.

Purpose

To help women stop missing out on their own life by treating themselves with the same kindness and respect they give to everyone else.

Vision

To live in a world where self-respect is not a luxury, but the daily standard from which people make decisions, build habits and lead their lives.

Values

  • Presence
  • Awareness
  • Integrity
  • Longevity

My Approach

Most of the women I work with are already doing too much, so my work is not about adding more to your life. It's about being assertive with your choices. I look at how you treat your body, how you talk to yourself, the environment you have built, the relationships you nourish, and the parts of yourself you have set aside.

Multicultural Perspective

Having lived across six countries and multiple cultures, I developed a particular understanding of what happens when you build your identity around trying to fit in and belong.

Self-Respect in Practice

Self-respect is not a reward. It is a standard built through daily decisions: the things you tolerate, the habits you reinforce, and the way you respond to your own needs.

Reduction of Toxins

We identify and reduce the physical, emotional, relational and behavioural toxins that drain your energy and compromise your wellbeing.

I guide you from overwhelm, self-criticism and disconnection
towards a life with more clarity, confidence and self-respect.
