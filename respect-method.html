The Respect Method

A twelve-week 1:1 coaching programme to help you live more intentionally, in line with who you are and what genuinely matters to you.

Most approaches try to change behaviour without taking into account your context, your emotional patterns, your environment or the relationship you have with yourself. The Respect Method works with all of these.

We work across body, mind, emotions, environment, habits, relationships and decision-making to create changes that hold up in your real life, not just on paper.

This isn't about chasing perfection, extreme productivity or control. It's about building enough self-respect to create a life that doesn't ask you to abandon yourself just to keep going.

What is included:

  • 12 x 60-minute weekly sessions
  • Pre-programme questionnaire
  • Written summaries after each session
  • Access to digital resources
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • Personalised strategy built around your reality

Each session starts from your actual week: the decisions you made, the challenges you faced, the patterns that showed up. The aim isn't to create dependency on the process, but to build the autonomy to sustain changes consistently.

The Journey

The phases below are each step we move through together, in the order and pace that makes sense for your life, your reality, your context and your goals.

The Foundation

Before we begin, you'll complete a questionnaire covering your health, your routine, your habits, your goals and what you're currently finding difficult. In our first session, we go through it together so I can better understand your reality, and you can begin to see it a little more clearly too.

The Shift

We look at the patterns, beliefs, habits and dynamics that have been keeping your current way of living in place. We pay attention to what's creating friction in your relationships, your routine, your body, your environment and in the way you speak to yourself.

The Practice

This is where self-knowledge becomes something practical. Together, we make more intentional choices about food, movement, rest, boundaries, routines, relationships and how you use your energy. All of it shaped around your actual life, not a generic blueprint.

The Roadmap

In our final sessions, we bring the whole process together into a clear, personal strategy for the months ahead. You leave with more clarity about what truly matters to you, how to keep these changes going in practice, and how to keep choosing yourself even when life doesn't go as planned.

Is this programme for you?

Probably yes, if you are willing to:

  • Participate with honesty and consistency
  • Build changes gradually and sustainably
  • Prioritise your health and wellbeing more intentionally
  • Develop a more respectful relationship with yourself

Probably not, if you want:

  • A restrictive meal plan or rigid rules
  • Quick, superficial solutions
  • Someone to tell you exactly what to do without reflection or active participation
  • Changes built solely on pressure and control
