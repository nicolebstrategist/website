The Respect Method

A 12-week private mentoring partnership built on the two things most wellbeing programmes skip.

Most programmes hand you a ready-made plan and tell you what to do. But they don't take into account:

  • Your history, your culture, your beliefs
  • Your identity, personality, context and life reality
  • The toxic load in your environment, your food, your cosmetics, your products and your relationships

A plan designed for someone else, in a life that looks nothing like yours, is never going to work. Not because you lack discipline or motivation. Because it was never actually built for you.

The Respect Method doesn't fit you into a programme. It builds the programme around you, taking into account who you are, where you live, your context and the relationships that are part of your reality.

What's included:

  • 12 x 60-minute weekly sessions
  • A pre-programme questionnaire
  • Written session summaries after each conversation
  • Access to digital resources and tools
  • WhatsApp/email support Monday to Friday
  • A personalised wellbeing strategy built around your life, not a template

Every session opens with a review of your week: what you set out to do and what actually happened. No pressure or judgement. The structure is designed to keep you moving forward between sessions and to build the autonomy to keep growing long after the programme ends.

You've tried. Genuinely tried.

The diets, the challenges, the fresh starts that never quite became new chapters. The version of yourself that was so consistent, until suddenly, you weren't.

And now you're here. Not because you're weak or undisciplined. But because nothing you've tried has lasted or put an end to the dissatisfaction you feel.

— But inside, there's a persistent sense that you're not quite in the driver's seat. That you're just going with the flow rather than choosing the direction you're heading. That the gap between how you live and how you want to live just quietly keeps growing.

— But usually, our dissatisfaction with our body is just a reflection of something deeper. A real issue that's harder to name: a feeling of being out of control, a cycle you can't seem to break, an exhaustion that goes deeper than sleep could fix.

You've been trying harder and harder at solutions that treat the symptom. And you're tired. Not just of the results not lasting, but of the effort it takes to keep trying and failing.

It's not a lack of discipline. It's that you've been trying to change without knowing yourself first, and without looking at what's been weighing on you from the inside and the outside.

When the foundation becomes solid:

  • You finally understand what's actually been driving your patterns. Not as a concept, but as a clear, specific map of your behaviour, your triggers, your history and context. That knowledge doesn't just feel good. It shapes what you do next.
  • The cycle of restriction and collapse loses its grip. Not because you've found a stricter version of willpower, but because you've stopped fighting yourself. You're finally making decisions that support yourself instead.
  • You stop measuring your worth against how you or your body looks on a given day. That's not a small shift. It reorganises how you make decisions, how you speak to yourself, how you move through the world.
  • The changes you make are ones you can actually keep. Not because you're more disciplined or motivated, but because they're built on self-respect rather than self-criticism. Rather than punishing yourself into a better life, you're consciously choosing one and talking yourself kindly into it.
  • You start becoming your best version. Not by pushing harder, but by knowing yourself better, treating yourself more kindly, living a life coherent to your context and values, and finally making your own health and wellbeing a genuine priority.

Self-respect isn't a concept. It's a daily practice and the foundation of lasting transformation.

That's what we build inside the Respect Method.

The Journey

The Respect Method is a 12-week 1:1 mentoring programme for professionals who manage everything except themselves and who are ready, finally, to put themselves first. The phases below represent each step we move through together, in the order and pace that makes sense for your life, your reality, your context and your goals.

The Foundation

Before we begin, you'll complete a pre-programme questionnaire covering your health, your lifestyle, your habits, your goals and what you're hoping to change or achieve. In our first session, we go through your answers together so I can better understand your reality and you can begin to see it more honestly too.

The Shift

This is the core of the work. We examine the beliefs, habits and patterns that have been keeping you stuck. We look at what you're carrying: in your relationships, your body, your environment and in the way you talk to yourself. And we begin replacing what no longer serves you with something more solid: self-knowledge, self-respect, a kinder and more honest relationship with yourself, and decisions that reflect who you truly are.

The Practice

This is where self-knowledge becomes strategy. Together we define intentional, values-aligned decisions about how you exercise, eat, rest, relate and invest your time and energy — decisions built around your real life, not a template.

The Roadmap

In our final sessions, we consolidate everything into a clear, personalised strategy for the months ahead: what truly matters to you and what you're committed to maintaining, how to sustain it in practice, and what to do when life gets in the way. You leave not with a generic plan to follow, but with the self-knowledge and self-respect to keep choosing yourself long after the programme ends.

Is this programme right for you?

It probably is if you're willing to:

  • Show up honestly and consistently, even when you “don't feel like it”
  • Trust a process that works differently from anything you've tried before
  • Treat your own health and wellbeing as a genuine priority, not an afterthought

This probably isn't if you want:

  • A restrictive meal plan, a demanding training programme, or a set of rules to follow
  • Someone to tell you what to do instead of working together to figure out what's best for you
  • Quick, ready-made results instead of building the foundation for lasting change, step by step
